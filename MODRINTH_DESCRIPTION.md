# Progression Rebalance

Gold gear worth wearing, enchantments worth choosing, minecarts worth building, and potions worth brewing.
Minecraft's existing systems, rebalanced so that more of them are useful.

## What is Progression Rebalance?

Progression Rebalance is a Vanilla+ mod for Minecraft 1.21.1. It does not add new ores, tiers or progression
stages. Instead, it adjusts the numbers and rules of mechanics Minecraft already has, so that options most players
skip — gold equipment, rails, specialized enchantments, brewing, Luck — become real choices during a normal
playthrough.

Everything still looks and plays like Minecraft. Items keep their names and textures, and the enchanting table,
cauldron and villager screens work the way you expect.

## Highlights

**Useful gold equipment**
Gold becomes a fast, highly enchantable but fragile tier. Golden tools last four times longer and can mine
diamond ore. The golden sword hits harder than iron and swings faster than any other sword. Golden armor
protects better than iron but has no toughness and wears out sooner, so diamond is still the best at taking big
hits.

**Specialized enchantments that matter**
Protection is slightly weaker, so Fire, Blast and Projectile Protection are worth using when you know what you
are facing. Bane of Arthropods becomes **Bane** and also works against creepers, slimes, magma cubes, guardians and
ravagers. Thorns deals more damage and no longer blocks your own counter-attacks. Impaling also works on mobs
standing in water or rain.

**A better enchanting table**
Put more lapis in the table to see more offers. 1–3 lapis shows the normal offers, 4–6 lapis a second page and
7 or more a third. Pages are fixed until you enchant, so you cannot reroll by moving lapis around.

**Minecarts and rails that are worth building**
Minecarts reach 20 blocks per second on straight track and still take curves safely. Rails can be crafted from
copper, and powered rails give twice as many per craft.

**Villagers that don't skip the diamond age**
Armorers, toolsmiths and weaponsmiths still sell enchanted diamond gear, but they also ask for some diamonds. A
trading hall is a shortcut through diamond progression, not a way around it.

**Better potions**
Utility potions like Night Vision, Fire Resistance and Water Breathing last twice as long. Combat potions like
Strength and Regeneration last 50% longer, and level II potions are unchanged. Drinkable potions stack to 4.

**Mixed potions**
Pour up to three potions into a water cauldron and bottle them as a single Mixed Potion. The durations are
averaged and effect levels never change, so mixing saves inventory space without making potions stronger.

**Structure loot with a theme**
Structure chests get extra, fitting loot: fire resistance and blaze powder in Nether fortresses, water breathing
in shipwrecks and ocean ruins, minecarts and lanterns in mineshafts, and more. Nothing from vanilla loot is
removed. Rare chests can hold a new Potion of Resistance, and witches can drop a brewed potion.

**Luck that does something**
The Luck effect now gives extra rolls in structure chests and better odds on rare mob drops. A Potion of Luck
before raiding a structure finally pays off.

## Philosophy

Instead of adding ten new tiers or forcing artificial progression gates, Progression Rebalance tries to make
Minecraft's existing mechanics interact better. It prefers improving an overlooked option over weakening a strong
one, and it avoids new materials, skill trees and level locks.

## Compatibility and requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.1**
- **Fabric Loader** 0.19.5 or newer
- **Fabric API** (required)
- Required on **both client and server**. In single player, just install it on your game.
- Works in existing worlds. Villagers that already have trades keep them.
- Data packs that replace loot tables or enchantments take priority over the mod's changes.

## Configuration

Most balance values can be changed in `config/progressionrebalance.properties`, which is created the first time
you launch the game. Each system can also be switched off. In multiplayer, use the same config on the server and
on every client.
